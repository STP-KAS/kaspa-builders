## 12 Oct 2026: topic 161, B1TCOIN S1LVER (read 12 Oct 05:51Z)

- [Topic 161](https://kas-smiths.org/t/a-non-fungible-covenant-family-before-a-unique-asset-kcc-exists-six-questions-from-a-game-built-on-the-kcc-20-reference/161) (post 411, 12 Oct 04:18:43Z, S1LVERTOSHI): "A non-fungible covenant family before a unique-asset KCC exists — six questions from a game built on the KCC-20 reference". He is building B1TCOIN S1LVER, "a multi-year replay of Bitcoin's history as a game": a vault covenant releases a fixed-supply KCC-20 token (21,000,000, 8 decimals) on Bitcoin's block schedule sixteen years behind, each release drawn among holders of "M1NERs" (a covenant family with per-UTXO state, weighted by hashpower); the seed is BLAKE2b of a Kaspa block's sequencing commitment and the release index. Genesis "planned for late 2026, launch 3 January 2027".
- His claims, not desk-checked: the token is "the KCC-20 reference contract verbatim", their SilverScript toolchain compiles it to the published template hash, and "it ran 13 live releases and holder spends on testnet-10 this week"; the test build runs "under decoy names on testnet-10"; a test suite of "740 debugger tests, a mutation gate". No repo, txid or template hash is given in the post.
- The six questions go to the KCC authors (unique-asset KCC; KCC-1/KCC-2 conventions for a non-standard family; a one-way hand-over in a covenant-ID authority slot; an unbiased sampling primitive and i64 width; discovery via KCC-23 metadata and a covenant-ID UTXO lookup; the OpChainblockSeqCommit window). No replies at this read. Post [412](https://kas-smiths.org/t/fungible-token-covenant-specification-kcc20/8/77) (04:22:05Z) points to it from the KCC-20 topic.
- **The master keeps:** the six KCC questions as an open forum item in its Kas-Smiths row; the game is here.
