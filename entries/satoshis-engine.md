### README Now row "Satoshi's Engine" (whole row)

[kaspaexplained.com/satoshis-engine.pdf](https://kaspaexplained.com/satoshis-engine.pdf), by Moose.kas (@THEMOOSEISLOOS5). 89 pages. The PDF metadata says it was created 21 Sep 2026; no version is stated. sha256 `4062170b0d53aece…`. Read 26 Sep 2026. An essay on proof-of-work mining economics: the boom-and-bust cycle between coin price and mining cost, cost of production as a floor under price, and two one-way trends (more work behind each coin, less energy per unit of work). It names no network (p. 6) and makes no Kaspa protocol claim, so nothing in it conflicts with this board. Its main claim, that a coin costs more to produce each cycle, is reasoned rather than measured, and the author lists the fee handover and the demand floor as open questions (pp. 86–88).
